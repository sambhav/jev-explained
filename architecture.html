HOW IT WORKS / FROM FIRST PRINCIPLES

From input processing to a decision output.

A text-generating model does two jobs: process what you sent, then produce an answer a piece at a time. For a question with known answers, a decision model can expose numerical judgments without writing out an explanation.

Walk one ticket through both paths

Both paths answer the same three questions. Move through the four stages to compare their output formats. The stages are not a clock or a measured speed comparison.

The vocabulary behind the comparison

Token

A piece of text used by the model. It might be a word, part of a word or punctuation.

Prefill

The input-processing phase of a typical generative LLM. It builds contextual representations before the first output token.

Autoregressive decoding

Generate one token, include it in the context, then choose the next. Those output steps depend on one another.

Readout

A numerical mapping from a model’s internal representation to scores or probabilities. A conceptual way to understand a decision output.

KV cache

Stored intermediate information that a transformer can reuse rather than recomputing earlier context.

Calibration

Whether the probabilities match observed frequencies of correctness over many examples. It is measured over a dataset, not established by a single answer.

Why not just use structured output?

ApproachWhat it gives youWhat to compare
Rules or a small classifierEfficient decisions in a narrow, stable domainAccuracy and maintenance as inputs change
LLM with a strict output schemaBroad language ability with a constrained answer formatTask quality, output length, latency, price
LLM with candidate scoringA decision baseline using scores for allowed outcomesHow scores are obtained and calibrated for your task
JevA service specialized for typed judgments and distributionsThe same task, quality bar and end-to-end workflow

Typed output is useful, but it does not alone establish better reasoning or trustworthy probabilities. A short constrained LLM response may be far faster than a long reasoning response. Use the best reasonable baseline for your product.
