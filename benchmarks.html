How to interpret results on a different workload

  1. Define the task. Use the same input, permitted actions and success criteria for every candidate.
  2. Match the baseline to the job. Include your current system, a fast structured-output LLM and a narrow classifier if appropriate.
  3. Measure user-visible time. Report p50 and p95 including network, tools, retries and fallbacks.
  4. Measure useful work. Track correct automated outcomes, escalation rate and cost per successful task.
  5. Check uncertainty. Evaluate calibration and errors across your important customer segments.
